======================================================================
MEDIUM-SPECIFIC PREPROCESSING SUMMARY
======================================================================

This preprocessing splits the original dataset into three subsets
based on corrosive medium, then applies independent preprocessing
(cleaning, train/val/test split, scaling) to each subset.

PURPOSE:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Train medium-specific Random Forest and SVR models
2. Compare performance vs. general model (trained on all mediums)
3. Analyze how feature importance varies across corrosive environments
